Character design reference, photoreal, full-body lineup on a dusty Tatooine town street in harsh light of two suns: three original alien municipal street sweepers in identical uniforms — bright orange work vests with silver reflective stripes over dark-blue work overalls, dark-blue caps, work gloves. The back of every vest carries large white Cyrillic print in two lines: «ГБУ ЖИЛИЩНИК» / «РАЙОНА ТАТУИНА» (exact spelling). Left: FOREMAN, a tall thin blue-grey alien with a long neck and two drooping antennae; middle: a short stocky green reptilian with a thick tail; right: a round purple alien with four eyes and tiny legs. Two stand three-quarter back to camera so the back print is clearly readable, the foreman faces camera. Each holds a light broom: long matte-black handle, chrome emitter head, a wide fan of glowing cyan energy bristles. Sand-coloured adobe walls and domes behind. No other text, no logos, no watermark.
